# Conversion Optimization

## When to use
When editorial content needs to drive a specific user action — signing up, creating a hackathon, submitting an idea, or joining a team.

## Patterns

**Action-first copy** — lead with the outcome, not the feature. On `Ideas.tsx` empty states, write "Submit your first idea" not "No ideas have been submitted yet."

**Reduce decision cost** — in tooltips and onboarding nudges, remove optionality from the copy. "Add a team name" converts better than "You can optionally name your team."

**Match copy to phase** — activation copy (first hackathon) uses encouraging language; adoption copy (repeat use) uses efficiency language. Don't swap them.

## Pitfalls
Avoid measuring conversion on copy that ships without a linked feature. If the call-to-action points at a page that isn't live, the metric is meaningless. Close the brief only when both the copy and the feature are merged.
